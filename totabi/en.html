Totabi: Travel Journal & Diary

Scrapbook, Stickers & Stamps

Totabi is a travel journal for iPhone and iPad that looks and feels like a real leather notebook: pages turn and curl, the paper ages gently, and everything sits on the page like the real thing. Keep your trip in words, sketches, stamps, stickers, and little keepsakes.

Coming soon to the App Store

A notebook that feels real

Write and sketch with Apple Pencil using a fountain pen, calligraphy pen, gel pen, fineliner, pencil, or highlighter. Type in beautiful fonts or use a font made from your own handwriting.

Collect the little things along the way

Photograph Japan's eki station stamps and Totabi lifts the ink off the paper. Turn any photo into a sticker, then add tickets, postmarks, pressed leaves, and washi tape. A trip map and a back pocket keep more of your journey close.

A diary for every day

Diaries give every day its own page, with writing prompts and a daily reminder to help you remember. Kitte, Totabi's postage-stamp mascot, has moods of its own. Face ID keeps your journals private.

Made for iPhone Duo and iPad

On unfolded iPhone Duo, the two-page spread follows the fold. Half-folded, the page stands up while the lower half becomes a desk. On iPad, your journal opens into a generous two-page spread.

Totabi Plus

Start free with three journals. Totabi Plus is available monthly, yearly, or for a lifetime, and adds unlimited journals, iCloud Sync, shiny sticker finishes, your own diary page design, and voice notes. Everything you make stays yours without Plus.

Private by design

Totabi has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. Your journals stay on your device or in your private iCloud.
